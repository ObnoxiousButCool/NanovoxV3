Outcome measurement for items that reached monitoring or completion.

This is the question the platform cannot answer yet. Measuring whether an intervention improved an account needs a before-and-after window per account, and batch 1 gives 85 of 92 employers a single call. The workflow is built so the measurement exists once the corpus does.

Sources are corpus-derived: 12 process-gap proposals, 11 knowledge failures, 4 escalation misses, 7 control exceptions and 4 exit-language calls from nanovox_v9_transcripts_batch1_001-100_1.pdf. Owning teams come from the Signal Taxonomy and Touchpoints sheets. Owners, assignment dates, due dates, actions taken and outcomes are prototype workflow data — the corpus carries no action records.
